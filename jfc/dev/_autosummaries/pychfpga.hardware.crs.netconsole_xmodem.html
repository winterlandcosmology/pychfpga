XMODEM sender over a connected netconsole UDP socket.

block_size selects classic XMODEM (128 / SOH) or XMODEM-1K (1024 / STX). U-Boot loadx accepts both.

pipe_send(filename, pipe_depth=8, timeout=60, pad=26, block_size=1024, ack_timeout=3.0, max_retries=10)

Pipelined XMODEM: pre-build batches of blocks, send, then collect ACKs.

Up to pipe_depth frames are transmitted back-to-back before waiting for their ACKs. On timeout/NAK/C, unacked frames of the batch are resent (U-Boot re-ACKs duplicates). Can overrun U-Boot’s netconsole input buffer (pipe_depth * (3 + block_size + 2) bytes must fit).

send(filename, timeout=60, pad=26, block_size=1024)

Standard XMODEM: build, send, and ACK-sync each block in turn.
